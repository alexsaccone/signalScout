# Design

SignalScout uses an ink-blue masthead, a cool white reading surface, and deep blue for interactive states. Amber signals stale data. Keep the workspace quiet enough to scan on a phone or a desktop.

Use a single sans-serif family, tabular numbers, and aligned table headings. The matchup view gives the two implied probabilities a shared split bar so their relationship is legible alongside the precise values. Use plain labels for quote expiry, sources, and unavailable data.
